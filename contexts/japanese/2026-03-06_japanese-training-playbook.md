## TABLE 1 — Core Methods Reference (What to do)

| Method | When to use | How to do it (rules) | Common mistake |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Shadowing** | Daily, short & intense | Play audio once, speak immediately, don’t pause, don’t correct | Turning it into pronunciation practice |
| **Massive Listening** | Daily, relaxed | Easy content, no pausing, follow meaning & emotion | Content too hard, background noise |
| **Reconstruction** | 3–5x/week | Listen → close input → explain in your own Japanese | Writing scripts, translating |

**Quick rule**

If your mouth moves without planning → correct use.

## TABLE 2 — Skills Map (What you’re strengthening)

### Listening Skills

| Skill | What to focus on | Self-check question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sound blocks | Fixed phrases & chunks | “Do I hear this as one unit?” |
| Boundaries | Where ideas end | “Where did the speaker pause?” |
| Prediction | Guess next phrase | “Was I surprised?” |
| Prosody | Emotion & intent | “Confident? Hesitant?” |
| Ambiguity tolerance | Keep going | “Did I stop because of one word?” |

---

### Speaking Skills

| Skill | What to practice | Self-check question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meaning-first | Decide message, not words | “What do I want to say?” |
| Pattern reuse | Sentence frames | “Did I reuse known forms?” |
| Fallback fluency | Fillers & repairs | “Did I recover smoothly?” |
| Speed | Quick response | “Did I hesitate long?” |

## TABLE 3 — Daily Schedule Template (2–3 hours)

### **Standard Day (2.5 hours)**

| Block | Time | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep listening | 30–40 min | One short audio, repeat 2–3 times |
| Massive listening | 30 min | Easy podcast / video |
| Read → speak | 30 min | Read short text → explain aloud |
| Free speaking | 20–30 min | Self-talk, opinion, summary |
| Light shadowing | 10–15 min | Familiar audio, end easy |

**Golden rule**

If energy drops → shorten, don’t skip.

---

### **Low-energy day (30–45 min)**

| Priority | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Easy listening |
| 2 | Light shadowing |
| 3 | Short self-talk |

Even this counts. Consistency > hero days.

## TABLE 4 — Measurement & Correction (Am I doing it right?)

### Method Check

| Method | Good signal | Bad signal | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shadowing | Flow, automatic | Pausing, perfection | Slow audio, move on |
| Listening | Predicting | Noise, overload | Easier content |
| Reconstruction | Fast, simple | Silent, stuck | Simplify message |

---

### Weekly Progress Check

| Question | Yes = good |
| --- | --- |
| Do I understand faster? | ✔ |
| Do I freeze less? | ✔ |
| Do I recover faster? | ✔ |

If **2 out of 3** are yes → continue.

---

## TABLE 5 — “What to do when stuck” Cheat Sheet

| Problem | Immediate action |
| --- | --- |
| Can’t understand | Ignore details, follow emotion |
| Can’t speak | Use filler + simple phrase |
| Too tired | Switch to easy listening |
| Doubting method | Check speed, not accuracy |
